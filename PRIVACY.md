# Privacy Policy for Yes or No Extension

**Last updated:** October 2026

The **Yes or No** extension is committed to user privacy.

### 1. Data Collection
The Yes or No extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information or user data of any kind.

### 2. Permissions
This extension requests **zero special permissions** and has no access to:
- Your browsing history or tabs
- The websites you visit
- Your personal files or cookies
- Any network or remote servers

### 3. Local Execution
All decision-making calculations happen 100% locally on your device within your browser session using standard cryptographic random functions. No question inputs or decisions are ever logged or uploaded.

### 4. Third-Party Services
This extension contains no analytics, no advertising networks, and no external third-party tracking scripts.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this extension, please reach out via GitHub repository issues.
